### 2. Purpose and target audience

BOOMER GO is a store operations and AI marketing application for authorized BOOMER OFF headquarters staff, employees, independently operated franchise stores, and other authorized store members in the BOOMER OFF retail network.

It solves the operational problem of store teams having to use disconnected tools for product identification, training knowledge, store tasks, marketing production, asset management, and content publishing. BOOMER GO provides one permission-controlled workspace for these workflows.

Public App Store distribution is required because authorized users work for multiple independently operated franchise entities and use their own iPhones. A normal App Store installation and update path is the most practical way to support those authorized users. The app is not limited to employees of a single legal entity, although access to business data requires an approved account and store assignment.

### 3. Access and setup instructions

No sample files or external hardware are required.

1. Launch BOOMER GO.
2. Select the username/password sign-in method.
3. Use the active review credentials provided in the Sign-in Information fields in App Store Connect.
4. After sign-in, the Home screen displays the assigned store and the main operational modules.
5. For product recognition, open AI Product Recognition and take a photo or choose a photo from the library. Camera and photo-library permissions are requested only when the reviewer selects those actions.
6. For AI marketing, open Marketing Center and choose BOOMER Video or Asset Library. The review account contains prepared sample assets so the reviewer can inspect completed results without waiting for a new video-rendering job.
7. Open Knowledge Base to view store training and product knowledge.
8. Open Me > Privacy, Help & Account Deletion to view the privacy policy, support channel, and initiate account deletion.

The internal community does not currently provide a user-to-user blocking feature. It is not a public social network: only approved BOOMER OFF users can sign in, and authorized administrators can remove inappropriate posts through the moderation console.

The review account is approved and has access to the application's main features. No one-time SMS code is required when the supplied password-login method is used.

### 4. External services and platforms

BOOMER GO uses the following external services to provide core functionality:

- Lovable Cloud's Supabase-compatible services for authentication, database access, object storage, realtime updates, and serverless functions.
- Tencent Cloud for web delivery, background worker services, media processing/storage, and mainland-China SMS delivery.
- Volcengine Ark / Seedance for AI video generation.
- DeepSeek for AI-assisted marketing script generation.
- Apple Push Notification service for optional notifications.
- The social-media platforms selected by an authorized store user for account connection and content publishing. These connections are initiated by the user and are not required to review the app's other features.

No external payment processor is used because the app does not sell digital content or services.

### 5. Regional behavior

The core authenticated app functions consistently in all regions where the app is available. The product is currently presented in Simplified Chinese and is designed for BOOMER OFF stores in mainland China. Mainland-China SMS login supports +86 mobile numbers; reviewers can use the supplied username/password login in any region. Store addresses, operating information, and marketing content vary by the store assigned to the authorized account.

### 6. Regulated industries and protected material

The app does not provide medical, financial, gambling, news, religious, or other highly regulated services. Baomu (Shanghai) Brand Management Co., Ltd. owns or is authorized to use the BOOMER OFF and BOOMER GO brand materials shown in the app. Store users upload their own product and store media for internal operations and authorized marketing use.
